# Privacy and cookies

This page covers the Artalk website and documentation at artalk.js.org. The site stores preferences such as your language and color theme in your browser. If you post a comment, the comment service receives your comment, the profile details you enter, your IP address and your browser’s user agent.

## Site analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to see which pages people visit and where they click or scroll. Microsoft uses this data to generate heatmaps and session recordings. We mask the comments section in recordings and disable analytics cookies by default. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we skip Clarity. See the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

## Advertising

We use Google AdSense to help fund Artalk. When ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and other identifiers to deliver and measure ads. They may use information about visits to this and other websites to personalize ads. Read [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where a consent message is shown, you can accept, decline or manage your choices. To change a choice later, use the privacy settings link Google displays at the bottom of the page. You can also manage personalized ads in [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and third-party advertising preferences at [YourAdChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/).

For questions about this website, contact [artalkjs@gmail.com](mailto:artalkjs@gmail.com).
